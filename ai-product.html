Zero to one product development

Product name

Research, development, deployment and evaluation of one healthcare AI product.

01Research02Development03Deployment04Evaluation & Iteration
PHASE 01

Research

Build the evidence base for the product across the people, organizations and market conditions that will determine its value and adoption.

01 · PATIENTS

Patients

Understand the problem through patient experiences, needs, journeys and behaviors.

Research structure

  • Needs and unmet needs
  • Patient journey
  • Behaviors and decision making
  • Trust, usability and expected outcomes
PHASE 02

Development

Turn the research evidence into a defined, testable and technically viable product.

01

Product Definition

Translate evidence into a clear value proposition and product requirements.

  • Use cases
  • Product requirements
  • Success criteria
02

Experience & Prototype

Design the product flow and make the proposed experience testable.

  • User flows
  • Prototype
  • Content and interaction
03

AI & Data Design

Define data inputs, model behavior, human oversight and technical constraints.

  • Data requirements
  • Model and system design
  • Safety boundaries
04

MVP Validation

Test value, usability and feasibility before committing to scale.

  • Concept validation
  • Usability testing
  • MVP scope
PHASE 03

Deployment

Introduce the product into a real care environment with the operational and governance structures required for use.

01

Pilot

Test the product with a bounded population and defined operational scope.

  • Site and cohort
  • Pilot protocol
  • Readiness criteria
02

Integration

Connect the product to workflows, systems and responsible teams.

  • Workflow integration
  • Technical integration
  • Support model
03

Governance

Establish ownership, oversight, escalation and monitoring.

  • Clinical oversight
  • Privacy and risk
  • Incident response
04

Launch

Prepare people, processes and communications for sustained use.

  • Training
  • Change management
  • Launch plan
PHASE 04

Evaluation & Iteration

Measure what the product does in practice and use the evidence to decide what changes next.

01

Adoption

Measure reach, onboarding, continued use and workflow fit.

  • Activation
  • Retention
  • Drop-off
02

Product & AI Performance

Evaluate product reliability and AI behavior in real conditions.

  • Accuracy and failure modes
  • Human override
  • System performance
03

Outcomes

Assess patient, clinical, operational and business outcomes.

  • Experience outcomes
  • Care outcomes
  • Operational value
04

Iteration

Prioritize changes using evidence from use, performance and outcomes.

  • Research backlog
  • Product improvements
  • Next release
