Zero to one · Product development methodology

From an unmet need to a product people can use.

A research-led product development framework that reduces uncertainty before scale. Each phase produces evidence for the next decision, with deliberate returns to discovery and testing when assumptions do not hold.

Product progressionResearch and iteration loop
Phase 01

Frame

Decide what is worth investigating.

01

Opportunity

Identify the unmet need, market context and initial opportunity.

  • Context and landscape
  • Signals and constraints
02

Discovery

Understand behaviors, needs, journeys and existing alternatives.

  • User and stakeholder research
  • Competitive evidence
03

Problem Definition

Define the priority audience, problem and hypothesis of value.

  • Problem statement
  • Success criteria
Evidence checkpointIs the problem important, specific and supported by evidence? Weak assumptions return to opportunity framing or discovery.↶
Phase 02

Shape

Turn the problem into something testable.

04

Product Concept

Define the value proposition, use cases and experience principles.

  • Concept and feature hypotheses
  • Value proposition
05

User Flows

Map tasks, decisions and interactions across the experience.

  • Critical paths
  • Information architecture
06

Prototype

Make the concept tangible at the lowest useful level of fidelity.

  • Wireframes
  • Interactive prototype
Learning loopConcept, flow and prototype evolve together. Findings may change the interaction, the product proposition or the definition of the problem itself.↶
Phase 03

Validate

Test value and usability before building.

07

Concept Validation

Evaluate relevance, comprehension, trust and intention to use.

  • Value signals
  • Objections and expectations
08

Usability Testing

Observe whether people can complete the critical tasks.

  • Behavior and breakdowns
  • Severity and priority
09

MVP Definition

Select the smallest coherent product that tests the core hypotheses.

  • Scope and tradeoffs
  • Measurement plan
Go, revise or stopThe evidence supports an MVP, sends the team back to reshape the solution, or shows that the opportunity should not proceed.↶
Phase 04

Deliver

Build, release and learn in context.

10

Build

Develop the product while preserving research intent and experience decisions.

  • Design quality checks
  • Implementation decisions
11

Beta & Launch

Observe real use, operational constraints, onboarding and adoption.

  • Beta feedback
  • Launch readiness
12

Measure & Iterate

Combine analytics, feedback and experiments to guide the next version.

  • Outcome measurement
  • Continuous discovery

Operating principles

Evidence before scopeDefine what must be learned before deciding what must be built.
Prototype before productionTest the riskiest assumptions at the lowest useful cost.
One decision per studyResearch questions must connect to a concrete product decision.
Launch starts learningReal-world behavior becomes evidence for the next product cycle.
